What does Narrativify do?

We provide organic social media account management, managed conversational marketing, conversation intelligence and creator/publisher distribution.

Which platforms do you support?

Supported channels include Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Threads, Reddit, Bluesky, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Snapchat and Telegram. Service availability and deliverables depend on platform capabilities and the selected scope.

What do you mean by human-led?

People research, review context, create or edit content, and make participation decisions. We do not use engagement bots, auto-commenting scripts or automated follow/unfollow systems to manufacture activity.

Does AI have a role?

AI tools may assist internal research or drafting where appropriate, but people review work for accuracy, brand voice, relevance and platform context. Human judgment and accountable execution remain central.

What is included in Social Media Account Management?

Depending on the plan: onboarding, brand voice and audience research, monthly calendar, platform-native content, publishing, graphics/carousels, relevant engagement, monitoring within agreed coverage, basic DM triage and monthly reporting. Exact limits are confirmed in the service agreement

What is Managed Conversational Marketing?

Researching relevant public conversations and supporting useful, contextual participation, outreach and qualified lead identification. We do not guarantee or manufacture likes, comments, shares, followers or sales

Are the 1,000+ team members active?

Narrativify states that it currently has 1,000+ active human team members. Larger campaign capacity may be organized for qualifying projects. The network figure is not a guarantee that every member will participate in every client campaign

Can campaigns scale to 3,000–10,000+ people?

For suitable large campaigns, Narrativify reports the ability to organize teams at that scale. Scope depends on availability, platform rules, campaign goals and safeguards. We do not deploy teams to create artificial endorsement signals

Do you guarantee results?

No. Social outcomes depend on the offer, market, creative quality, audience, platform algorithms and client follow-up. We agree on deliverables and reporting, not guaranteed sales or virality

Are creator or publisher fees included?

No. Third-party costs are estimates and are approved by the client before any commitment. Actual rates may vary

Is there an annual discount?

Yes. 10% off Narrativify’s current monthly plan price when paid annually upfront. Third-party distribution costs are excluded

How do I get started?

Contact us with your brand, goals, platforms and current challenges. We’ll recommend a suitable scope and confirm deliverables, approval workflow and response coverage before starting
