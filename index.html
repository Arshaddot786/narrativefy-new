Why human-led?

AI can produce words. People create context.

AI tools and software can speed up research and drafting. But social media is a people-to-people environment. A real person can interpret tone, understand community norms, recognize when not to reply, and build a relationship instead of simply triggering an action.

That is why our services are designed around human judgment and accountable execution. We do not use engagement bots, auto-commenting scripts or automated follow/unfollow systems to manufacture activity.

What human-led means at Narrativify

  • People review context before participating.
  • Content is adapted to the platform and brand voice.
  • Engagement is relevant—not copy-paste spam.
  • Interested prospects are handed to the client clearly.
  • Campaign scope and reporting are agreed in advance.
